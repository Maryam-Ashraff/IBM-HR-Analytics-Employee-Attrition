Step 1 — Business Problem
Understanding why employees leave and what drives turnover

High employee turnover increases hiring and training costs while reducing productivity. This project identifies why employees leave and helps HR predict and reduce attrition through data-driven insights.

Core Question
What is the current attrition rate?

Establishing a baseline attrition rate (16.0%) to quantify the scale of the problem and measure the impact of future interventions.

Risk Factors
Which factors drive employees to leave?

Identifying the strongest predictors — OverTime, Salary, Job Satisfaction, DistanceFromHome — that correlate with employee departure.

Role Analysis
Which departments have the highest turnover?

Pinpointing high-risk job roles (Sales Representatives, Lab Technicians) to target retention programs where they're needed most.

Step 3 — Data Quality Assessment
8 categories of quality issues identified before cleaning
1. Missing Values

Present in Age (2.9%), MonthlyIncome (8.0%), TotalWorkingYears (4.1%), YearsAtCompany (5.0%), EducationField (1.9%), and JobRole (1.0%).

2. High Missing Column

ManagerNotes has 60% missing values — effectively unusable for any analysis. Must be dropped entirely.

3. Constant Features

EmployeeCount, StandardHours, and Over18 have only 1 unique value across all 1,495 rows — zero predictive variance.

4. Inconsistent Text

Extra whitespaces in Gender (" Male "), inconsistent categories in BusinessTravel (Travel_Rarely vs Travel-Rarely vs TRAVEL_RARELY).

5. Invalid Values

Age ranges from 16 to 78 (out-of-range). DistanceFromHome max is 150. MonthlyIncome reaches 500,000 (scale error).

6. Outliers (IQR)

MonthlyIncome (105 outliers), YearsSinceLastPromotion (109), YearsAtCompany (67), TotalWorkingYears (63) — significant statistical outliers detected.

Step 7 — Feature Engineering
5 new domain-specific features to improve model accuracy and business value

Beyond raw data preprocessing, 5 engineered features were crafted from domain knowledge to capture deeper behavioral patterns in the data.

💰

Income_per_Working_Year

MonthlyIncome / (TotalWorkingYears + 1)
Normalizes income by career experience to flag underpaid employees relative to seniority.

📊

Experience_Level

Junior (0–2y), Mid (3–7y), Senior (8–15y), Expert (15y+)
Groups career years into bands to analyze attrition by career stage.

🚀

Promotion_Rate

YearsSinceLastPromotion / (YearsAtCompany + 1)
Measures promotion delay relative to tenure — flags stagnant staff prone to departure.

📅

Tenure_Group

0-1yr, 1-3yr, 3-5yr, 5yr+
Groups tenure into behavioral buckets — new hires vs veterans have different attrition drivers.

🏷️

High_Income_Indicator

1 if MonthlyIncome > Median, else 0
Binary flag for fast compensation segmentation in reporting and tree models.

Step 8 — Executive Summary
One-page summary for senior, non-technical management
The Problem

Unplanned employee turnover threatens organizational stability — driving up replacement costs and reducing business productivity. The goal: identify core drivers of departure and equip leadership with targeted retention strategies.

Expected Impact

Reducing overall attrition from the current 16.0% down to 10.0% will save substantial recruitment and onboarding expenses annually. Retaining skilled staff preserves institutional knowledge and stabilizes client relationships.

⏰

OverTime Governance & Burnout Management

Restrict consecutive overtime days. Establish workload auditing. Introduce compensatory time-off or spot bonuses for peak project phases.

🎯

Targeted Compensation & Growth Strategy

Conduct market salary benchmarking for Sales Representatives and Lab Technicians. Create transparent 12-month career growth roadmaps for entry-level positions.

📊

Early Warning HR Intervention

Deploy quarterly pulse surveys on JobSatisfaction and EnvironmentSatisfaction. Trigger automated 1-on-1 stay interviews whenever satisfaction drops to 1 or 2.

💡 Suggested next steps for the organization▼

Pilot Program: Launch a 90-day overtime management pilot in the Sales department.
Predictive Modeling: Build and deploy a Machine Learning classification model (e.g., Random Forest / XGBoost) to generate early-warning attrition risk scores for active employees.
